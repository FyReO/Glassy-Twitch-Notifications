Welcome to Twitch Live Notifier

A small glass notification that tells you the moment someone you follow goes live.

  1. 1

    Connect your Twitch account

    Your browser opens once. Click Authorize and you're done. The app only reads who you follow.

  2. 2

    Get notified, then click

    When a streamer you follow goes live, a notification slides onto your screen. Click it to open their stream.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    The app runs quietly in the tray near your clock and starts with Windows. Right-click the icon and choose Settings to pick where it appears, how it moves, the sound, and who to mute.
